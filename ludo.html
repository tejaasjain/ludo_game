Ludo Rules

  • Roll a 6 to move a token out of the base.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you an extra turn.
  • Tokens follow the colored path to reach the center home.
  • Land on an opponent's token to capture it and send it back to base (gives extra turn).
  • Star cells are safe zones where tokens cannot be captured.
  • The first player to get all 4 tokens to the center wins!
